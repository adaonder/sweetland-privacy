Rules that apply to all SweetLand apps

  • We do not sell your personal data and do not rent it out for marketing.
  • We never ask for your real name, phone number, contacts or precise location.
  • We never see your payment card details. Where an App offers purchases, payment is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play Billing).
  • Settings and progress saved on your device stay on your device unless the section for that App says otherwise. Uninstalling an App, or clearing its data in your device settings, removes them.

Wolftrap

Package: com.AdaGames.Wolftrap · Contact: destekbendesup@gmail.com

Pishti – Online Card Game (Pişti Oyun Online)

Package: com.sweetland.poo · Contact: onderteldroid@gmail.com

Pishti is an online multiplayer card game. It uses an account (guest or e-mail), shows ads and offers optional purchases.

Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in: you can play as a guest; an anonymous identifier specific to your device is created and is not linked to your personal details. Optionally you can create an account with an e-mail address and password to carry your progress across devices.
  • Profile and game data: the username and avatar you choose, your Elo rating, match history and leaderboard records.
  • Device information: the device identifier used to authenticate guest accounts, app version and language setting.
  • Advertising: the App shows ads between and after your free daily matches. Our advertising partners (Unity Ads / ironSource – LevelPlay Mediation) may process your device's Advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is collected by the advertising partner.
  • Purchases: if you buy in-app energy (match) packs, the transaction ID and product information are recorded to verify the purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play.

The App contains no usage-analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Before the advertising SDK starts for the first time you are asked for consent to personalised ads (GDPR); if you decline, ads are served without personalisation. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off personalisation in your device settings.

How we use it

  • to provide the core functions of the game (sessions, syncing progress across devices);
  • to calculate Elo ratings and leaderboards;
  • to prevent abuse, cheating and fake accounts;
  • to show ads and grant ad rewards (extra matches);
  • to process and verify in-app purchases;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

Third-party services and sharing

The game server (account, match and leaderboard data) runs on infrastructure operated by us (the open-source Nakama server); this data is not transferred to any third-party game or analytics platform.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account instantly and permanently inside the App: open Profile from the main menu, tap the red Delete Account button at the bottom ("Delete Guest" for guest accounts) and complete the two confirmation steps. Your account, username, avatar, Elo rating, match history and leaderboard records are then permanently deleted from our servers; this cannot be undone, and deleted data is not kept beyond the routine backup cycle. If you cannot access the App, e-mail us with the same request. You may also ask us what data we hold about you or to correct inaccurate data.

Memeshop

Package: com.sweetland.mememshop · Contact: firstdesteksuppport@gmail.com

Memeshop is a meme editor. Your photos and memes are edited on your device and are never uploaded to us. We do not ask for an account, name or e-mail. The only data that leaves your device is what our advertising partner (Unity Ads) and Google Play need to show ads and process optional purchases.

Photos, camera and the memes you create

  • When you add an image, the App uses the Android system photo picker (or the camera, if you choose to take a photo). The App only receives the single image you select; it does not have access to your whole gallery.
  • Images are processed only on your device. We do not upload, store or view your photos or memes on any server.
  • Memes you save are written to your device's Pictures folder. If you tap Share, the image is sent to the app you choose; that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Unity Ads may automatically collect and process: device identifiers including the Android Advertising ID; device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version); IP address and approximate location derived from it; and ad interaction data (ads viewed or clicked). This data is processed by Unity under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as the Pro version) are processed by Google Play Billing. The App only receives a confirmation of which product you own so it can unlock it.

Other

  • Settings and editor preferences (text styles, presets, etc.) are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not use any analytics service of our own.
  • Data deletion: we keep none of your data on our own servers. Remove local data by uninstalling the App or via Settings → Apps → Memeshop → Storage → Clear data. For data processed by Unity Ads, reset your Advertising ID or submit a request to Unity through the link above.
